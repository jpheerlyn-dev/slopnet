# Humans

Use this prompt format:
GOAL — what you want done.
WHERE — the files or area to touch.
DON'T — what must not change.
CHECK — how to prove it worked.

## Five habits

- Give one theme per prompt.
- Read the register first.
- Name things yourself.
- Give small models small tasks.
- Paste errors exactly as you saw them.

## Your jobs

- Give naming verdicts.
- Click the real app and check it works.
- Answer PENDING items.
